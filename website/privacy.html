Privacy Policy

Effective October 15, 2026

The short version: SwiftUI for Humans doesn't collect, store, or share any information about you. There are no accounts, ads, analytics, or tracking.

What the app stores

The app saves a few things on your device so it works the way you left it: which checklist items you've ticked off, your appearance setting, and the last page you had open. This stays on your device. It's never sent to us or anyone else, and it's removed when you delete the app.

To decide when to ask for a rating or a tip, the app also counts, on your device, how many pages you've opened and on how many days you've used it. Those counts never leave your device either.

Tips

You can leave an optional tip as an in-app purchase. Apple handles the payment; we never see your name, card, or Apple Account. Apple's privacy policy covers purchases.

What the app doesn't do

  • It doesn't ask you to sign in or create an account.
  • It doesn't include analytics, advertising, or crash-reporting tools.
  • It doesn't track you across other apps or websites.
  • It doesn't ask for access to your location, contacts, photos, camera, or microphone.

Content loaded from the internet

A few parts of the app load content from other services. Like any website, these services receive your device's IP address and basic request details when they send it. We don't receive or see any of this.

  • Apple's websites. Links to Apple's documentation and guidelines open apple.com and swift.org pages inside the app. Apple's privacy policy applies to those pages. Links to other websites open in Safari.
  • Sample photos. The pages that show how to load an image from the web download sample photos from Lorem Picsum.
  • Maps. The Map page shows Apple Maps, which loads map data from Apple.

This website

This website has no cookies, analytics, or tracking. It's hosted by Vercel, which, like any web host, processes your IP address to deliver pages and protect the site. See Vercel's privacy policy.

Children

The app doesn't collect information from anyone, including children under 13.

Changes

If this policy changes, the new version will be posted here with a new effective date. If the app ever starts collecting data, this page will say so before that version is released.
