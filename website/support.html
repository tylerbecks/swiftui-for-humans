Support

Questions, bugs, mistakes, and ideas are all welcome.

Email tylerdbecks@gmail.com. I read everything and usually reply within a few days.

Found a mistake?

Apple's frameworks change every year, and a page can fall behind. Tell me which page it's on and what looks wrong, and I'll check it against Apple's documentation. A screenshot helps.

Something isn't working

Include your device (for example, iPhone 17, iPad Air, or MacBook Air), your iOS or macOS version, the app version (on iPhone and iPad, shown on the About screen at the bottom of each tab's list; on the Mac, choose SwiftUI for Humans > About SwiftUI for Humans), and what you tapped or clicked before it happened.

Want a page added?

Suggest any SwiftUI view, modifier, design topic, or App Store step you'd like explained.

Common questions

Which devices does it run on?
iPhone and iPad with iOS 26 or later, and Mac with macOS 26 or later. The Mac app has its own pages about building and shipping Mac apps.

Does it need an internet connection?
No. Everything works offline except Apple's documentation pages, a few sample photos, and the map example.

Is it made by Apple?
No. It's an independent project that links to Apple's documentation and Human Interface Guidelines and explains them in plain language.

Does it collect my data?
No. See the privacy policy.
